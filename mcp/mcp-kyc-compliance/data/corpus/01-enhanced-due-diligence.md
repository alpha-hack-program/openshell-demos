# Enhanced due diligence

A transaction warrants enhanced due diligence review when its amount or
pattern deviates significantly from the client's declared historical
activity — for example, a transfer far larger than the client's typical
activity, an unusually rapid sequence of transactions, or activity that
does not match the client's stated occupation or sector of interest.

Enhanced review is especially warranted when the transaction involves a
higher-risk jurisdiction, or when the client carries a politically exposed
person (PEP) flag. In either case, the reviewing banker should document
the reason the transaction was flagged and the outcome of the review
before allowing the transaction to proceed.
