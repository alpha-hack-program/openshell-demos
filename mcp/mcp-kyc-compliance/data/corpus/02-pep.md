# Politically exposed persons (PEP)

A client flagged as a politically exposed person (PEP) requires additional
oversight before certain operations proceed. Any operation that would
exceed the client's declared standard portfolio threshold requires prior
approval from a compliance officer — the banker of record may not approve
it unilaterally, regardless of how routine the operation would otherwise
be for a non-PEP client.

Approval must be accompanied by a documented source-of-funds review:
a brief written record of where the funds involved in the operation
originated, sufficient to support the compliance officer's approval
decision. The absence of a documented source-of-funds review is itself
grounds to withhold approval, even if the operation would otherwise be
unremarkable.
